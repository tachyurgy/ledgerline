1,200 orders · 5 merchants · liability-bearing policy

Deciding an ecommerce order in real time is not a fraud-detection problem, it is a pricing problem: whoever carries the chargeback also carries the cost of every good customer they turn away. This engine chains three specialist sub-models — address manipulation, IP and device, basket composition — into a consortium model that also sees cross-merchant velocity, then reports the exact log-odds contribution of every signal. Move the thresholds and watch net margin, not just fraud, respond.

Decision explanation

How it is built. model.js is four logistic regressions. Three specialists score their own signal family; their probabilities become features of the consortium model alongside signals only a network can see — the same card hitting five merchants in a day. Because the top model is linear in log-odds, attribution is exact: every bar is weight × value, and the bars plus the bias sum to z. Approximating that with a post-hoc explainer would be strictly worse and slower.

Orders are synthetic and seeded, but fraud is drawn from genuinely different signal distributions rather than by relabelling random rows — young identities, IP-to-billing distance, resale-liquid baskets, expedited shipping, cross-merchant velocity. A demo where the classes are not actually separable would be a lie about how well the model works.
